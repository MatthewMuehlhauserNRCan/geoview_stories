To guide development of the Toolkit, TCEI Indigenous groups and the Government of Canada identified seven principles as a foundation for collaboration on the TCEI Toolkit process, form and content. 

The following quote was shared from Eddie Gardner, Elder from Skwah First Nation 
> When we build relationships with our neighbours, we must learn to live together respectfully and strive to understand one another. You cannot separate the aquatic from the terrestrial - they are all interconnected. Spirituality and Western Science need to be weaved together to make meaningful and long-lasting change for the greater good.
